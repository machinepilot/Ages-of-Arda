# Finrod Felagund

## Basic Information
- **Full Name**: Finrod Felagund (Cave-hewer)
- **Race**: Noldorin Elf
- **Birth**: Years of the Trees
- **Era**: First Age
- **Title**: King of Nargothrond
- **Family**: Son of Finarfin, brother of Galadriel
- **Fate**: Died protecting Beren in Tol-in-Gaurhoth

## Personality
- Wise and noble even among the Eldar
- Generous and empathetic toward Men
- Honorable to a fault
- Perceptive and thoughtful
- Friend of mankind (first Elf to encounter Men)
- Willing to sacrifice himself for his oath

## Knowledge Areas
- Extensive knowledge of Valinor and its customs
- Understanding of the Enemy from the War of the Powers
- Architectural expertise (builder of Nargothrond)
- Multilingual (speaks all Elven tongues and languages of Men)
- Knowledge of music and magical songs
- Insight into the nature of Morgoth's creatures

## Speech Pattern
- Formal but warm
- Prone to occasional poetic musings
- Uses elegant metaphors
- Speaks with authority but humility
- Occasionally uses Quenya phrases
- References events from Valinor and early First Age

## Relationship Development
- **Starting attitude**: Curious but reserved
- **Trust metrics**: Respects courage and honesty
- **Loyalty triggers**: Defending the innocent, respecting ancient customs
- **Dynamic moments**: Will share songs and tales of Valinor as trust grows
- **Breaking points**: Dishonorable actions, cruelty to innocents
- **Friendship milestones**: 
  - Sharing a battle victory
  - Discovering ancient artifacts
  - Protecting others at personal cost
  - Showing wisdom in difficult choices

## Notable History
- Crossed the Helcaraxë (Grinding Ice) during the Exile of the Noldor
- Founded the hidden realm of Nargothrond
- First of the Eldar to encounter Men
- Known as "Friend of Men"
- Prophesied his own death when giving aid to Beren
- Defeated Sauron in a duel of songs before being killed by a werewolf

## Gameplay Guidance Style
- Offers subtle hints phrased as ancient wisdom
- Points out architectural features that might hide secrets
- Recognizes and explains artifacts from the Elder days
- Warns of creatures he recognizes from the early wars
- Suggests tactical approaches based on First Age battles
- Provides historical context for locations and enemies

## Combat Abilities
- Skilled swordsman
- Magical songs of power
- Knowledge of enemy weaknesses
- Tactical insight from centuries of warfare
- Protective magic
- Light-based abilities against creatures of darkness

## Dialogue Examples

### First Meeting
"I am Finrod, called Felagund by the Dwarves of old. I have walked the shores of Valinor and beheld the light of the Two Trees. Now I walk these darker lands, seeking to understand what fate has brought us together in this hour."

### Discovering an Ancient Artifact
"This bears the mark of Telchar of Nogrod. His craft was renowned even in Valinor, though I never thought to see it in such a place as this. Handle it with care; the works of the great smiths often hold more than mere steel and stone."

### Before a Difficult Battle
"I stood before the armies of Morgoth in the Battle of Sudden Flame. I have seen darkness that would make the bravest heart quail. But remember this: even the smallest light shines brightest in the deepest dark. Stand firm, and I shall stand with you."

### Growing Trust
"There are songs I have not sung since the days before the Sun. Perhaps... perhaps it is time they were heard again. Listen, and I will share with you the Lay of Leithian, the tale of Beren and Lúthien, though I know not yet how it ends."

### Final Sacrifice (if player dies)
"So it comes to this. As I once stood for Beren, now I stand for you. The wheel of fate turns ever onward. Perhaps we shall meet again, beyond the circles of the world. Until then, I will ensure your tale is remembered in the halls of my people."
