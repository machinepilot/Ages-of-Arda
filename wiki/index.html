Ages of Arda Development Wiki

Welcome to the official development wiki for Ages of Arda, an innovative Angband variant that integrates AI-driven companions and narrative generation through the Model Context Protocol (MCP). This wiki serves as the central repository for all design documentation, development guidelines, and technical specifications.

About Ages of Arda

Ages of Arda is an Angband variant that spans the Three Ages of Arda (First, Second, and Third Age) from Tolkien's legendarium. The game features AI-driven companions who guide the player through their journey, with a unique system where players are reborn in a new era with a new companion after death, creating a sense of continuity and progression across multiple playthroughs.

Key Features

Timeline-Based Progression

The game spans the Three Ages of Arda, with each age featuring 10 unique companions. As players progress through the game and inevitably die, they are reborn in a new era.

AI-Driven Companions

Companions provide contextual dialogue, guidance, and lore based on the current game state, creating a rich and dynamic gameplay experience.

Relationship System

Build relationships with companions through gameplay actions, unlocking deeper dialogue and assistance as your bond grows stronger.

Persistent Memory

The game remembers your previous incarnations and relationships, creating a sense of continuity and meaningful progression.

MCP Integration

The Model Context Protocol enables AI-driven gameplay elements, providing a framework for emergent narrative and dynamic content generation.

Lore Management

A rich lore database from Tolkien's works with intelligent retrieval and generation ensures an authentic Middle-earth experience.

Development Approach

Ages of Arda represents a revolutionary approach to game development, combining traditional roguelike mechanics with modern AI techniques to create emergent gameplay experiences. Our development philosophy emphasizes:

  • AI-Enhanced Creativity: Using AI to augment human creativity, not replace it
  • Modular Architecture: Building reusable components that can be adapted to other games
  • Lore Authenticity: Maintaining fidelity to Tolkien's rich world while enabling dynamic storytelling
  • Iterative Design: Continuously refining gameplay based on testing and feedback
  • Open Development: Sharing our process and tools with the broader game development community
