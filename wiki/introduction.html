Executive Summary

Step into the mythic Ages of Middle Earth with Ages of Arda, a groundbreaking roguelike that builds upon the classic Angband foundation through the lens of J.R.R. Tolkien's legendary world. This preview unveils a gameplay experience where:

  • Multiple Ages: Journey through the First, Second, and Third Ages with your actions in earlier periods affecting later ones.
  • AI-Powered Companions: Travel with context-aware NPCs who provide knowledge, combat assistance, and narrative opportunities unique to their background and your shared history.
  • Dynamic Narrative: Experience procedurally generated stories and quests that respond to your choices while staying true to Tolkien's lore.
  • Lore Authenticity: Explore a world built on a comprehensive database of Tolkien's works, ensuring consistency with the established mythology.
  • Emergent Gameplay: MCP (Model Context Protocol) integration creates responsive AI interactions through local language models for unpredictable but coherent adventures.

Set against the backdrop of Beleriand and beyond, Ages of Arda merges traditional roguelike challenge with AI-enhanced storytelling into an evolving journey through the ages of Middle-earth.

Game Concept

Ages of Arda represents a bold evolution of the roguelike genre built on the Angband codebase, expanding it to encompass Tolkien's entire legendarium. Unlike traditional roguelikes focused solely on dungeon crawling, Ages of Arda creates a temporally connected experience across the three Ages of Middle-earth, where your actions in each era affect subsequent ones.

What distinguishes Ages of Arda from other Tolkien-inspired games and traditional roguelikes:

Age Progression System

Play across the First, Second, and Third Ages with actions in earlier eras affecting later ones through the character reincarnation system.

AI-Driven Companions

Interact with NPCs powered by the Multi-Agent Framework, each with age-appropriate knowledge, personalities, and goals.

Lore Management System

Experience a world built on a comprehensive database of Tolkien's works, ensuring authenticity and consistency.

Model Context Protocol

Enjoy responsive, context-aware AI interactions through local language models that understand your gameplay history.

Memory Bank System

Benefit from a persistent repository that maintains your experiences, choices, and world-altering decisions across playthroughs.

Gameplay Overview

In Ages of Arda, you'll experience Tolkien's world across its three defining eras. Beginning in the First Age, you'll choose from races like the Noldor Elves, Sindar, Edain, or Dwarves, each with distinct abilities and cultural connections. As you journey through Middle-earth, your AI companions—drawn from various races and backgrounds—will accompany you, offering age-appropriate knowledge and support.

Press ENTER to begin your quest

When your character dies or completes major objectives in one age, you can advance to the next, with your actions and discoveries in earlier ages influencing the world state. Your companions will change to reflect the new era, but relationships with immortal beings may persist across ages.

First Age: The Elder Days

Experience the war against Morgoth in Beleriand, interact with legendary figures like Fingolfin and Beren, and witness events from The Silmarillion. This age focuses on epic heroism against overwhelming odds.

Second Age: The Age of Númenor

Navigate the era of the Rings of Power and the rise and fall of Númenor. Explore Lindon, Eregion, and early Gondor while dealing with Sauron's growing influence and political intrigue.

Third Age: The Familiar Era

Adventure in the world familiar from The Hobbit and The Lord of the Rings, with locations like Gondor, Rohan, and Mirkwood. Focus on small-scale heroism in a world of fading magic and spreading darkness.

A Typical Gameplay Session

  1. Character Creation: Select age, race, class, and customize your AI companion.
  2. World Exploration: Traverse the regions of Middle-earth appropriate to your chosen age.
  3. Companion Interaction: Develop relationships with AI-driven companions who provide context-aware assistance.
  4. Lore Discovery: Uncover canonical and procedurally generated lore that enriches your understanding of Tolkien's world.
  5. Dynamic Quests: Engage with narratives that adapt to your choices and the current age.
  6. Age Transition: Upon death or completion of major objectives, advance to the next age while carrying forward your legacy.

The Companion System works through the Model Context Protocol, connecting the game engine to local large language models via Ollama. This allows companions to generate contextually appropriate dialogue and behavior while maintaining consistent personalities. All processing happens locally on your machine, ensuring responsive gameplay without network latency.

Conclusion

Ages of Arda represents an ambitious expansion of the roguelike genre, merging the tactical depth of ASCII-based gameplay with the rich lore of Tolkien's world and modern AI capabilities. The core innovation is the spanning of three ages, allowing players to experience the unfolding history of Middle-earth with their actions rippling through time.

As a community-driven open-source project, Ages of Arda invites contributors to help shape both the game and its innovative AI architecture. The upcoming alpha will focus on the First Age implementation, providing a foundation for the multi-age experience to come.
