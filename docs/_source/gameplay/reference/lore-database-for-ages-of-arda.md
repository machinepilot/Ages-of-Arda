# Lore Database for Ages of Arda

This directory contains lore extracted from Tolkien's works for use in the Ages of Arda companion system.

## Structure

- `lore_index.json`: Index of all books in the lore database
- `first_age/`: Lore from the First Age
- `second_age/`: Lore from the Second Age
- `third_age/`: Lore from the Third Age

## Books

- The Hobbit (Third Age, Year 2941)
- The Lord of the Rings (Third Age, Year 3018)
- The Silmarillion (First Age, Year 1)
